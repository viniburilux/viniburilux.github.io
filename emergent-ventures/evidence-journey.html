The experiment

I did not start with a lab. I started with questions.

The public record below is an attempt to make an unusual trajectory inspectable: what was built, what was accepted, what remains experimental, and where a reader can verify the underlying artifacts.

The broader operating model is called LUXVERSO Research & Build. Its domains change — multi-model AI, territorial intelligence, biotechnology, public data, scientific discovery — but the recurring work is to turn difficult questions into representations, experiments, systems, and documented next questions.

Why this experiment matters

AI and open infrastructure may be lowering some of the historical barriers between a curious person and a serious attempt to investigate and build. They do not make institutions, expertise, or reality checks unnecessary. They change the amount of leverage available before those resources are present.

What becomes possible in the space between an individual and an institution?

This public record is one answer in progress.
